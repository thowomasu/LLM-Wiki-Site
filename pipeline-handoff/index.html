What changed?

The wiki has been reorganized into a more professional research format. Instead of one long technical note, the work is split into plain-language pages for progress, findings, implementation, and evidence.

The main research direction added this week is the Open Knowledge Format idea: the site can stay readable for people, while a structured OKF layer can make the same knowledge easier to reuse later in tools, pipelines, and agent workflows.
