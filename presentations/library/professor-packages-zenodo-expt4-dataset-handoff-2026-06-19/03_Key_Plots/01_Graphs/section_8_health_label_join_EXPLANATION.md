# Graph Explanation: combined EIS and time-domain DRT prototype Health Label Join

Graph file: `section_8_health_label_join.png`

## What This Figure Shows

This graph joins fitted GITT DRT-like features to LG M50T 21700 Expt 4 drive-cycle aging health labels.

## How To Read It

Check whether fitted R0 and broad DRT band mass move with SoH, 0.1s resistance, and charge throughput. Read colors by temperature group before trusting any global trend.

## What We Can Learn

This is the first useful ageing-label diagnostic in the package. It tells us whether the DRT-like outputs are worth testing as health features.

## Caveat

Correlation is not validation. Cross-temperature pooling can fake a strong relationship, so the next step must be held-out-cell testing inside temperature groups.
