# Mini Presentation: Key Plots

## Slide 1: Results

- This folder collects the visual evidence from the LG M50T 21700 Expt 4 drive-cycle aging pipeline.
- The plots summarize data audit, GITT windows, fits, batch behavior, model sensitivity, raw conversion, health labels, validation, trend consistency, discharge features, hybrid features, and multi-protocol validation.
- The visuals support fast review without opening every CSV.

---

## Slide 2: Achievements

- Key figures are separated from the full report tree.
- Sidecar explanations and graph indexes make each plot easier to interpret.
- The folder helps a professor quickly see what worked and where the claims weaken.

---

## Slide 3: Open Issues

- Plots do not remove leakage risk.
- Good-looking prediction plots can still be scientifically weak if target proxies are included.
- No plot here proves EIS-validated DRT peaks.
