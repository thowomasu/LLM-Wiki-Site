# LLM Wiki Site

The LLM Wiki Site is the public/human-readable layer of the project. It
contains research narratives, dataset status pages, validation outputs,
presentation pages, source links, plots, and pipeline evidence.

## Current Role

- Preserve research outputs in Git.
- Provide readable pages for professor/team review.
- Keep claims, validation status, and limitations visible.
- Act as the first layer that can later be mirrored into an OKF knowledge
  bundle.
