August dataset work: a focused TS + EIS + DRT queue

This update records a completed source-level screen and the remaining August work. A candidate is not called a matched dataset until its files prove a common cell, test, cycle, SOC, temperature, or protocol key.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

Completed on 22 August

Four sources were screened against the pipeline requirements

  • Time-series evidence: current, voltage, time, cycling, or pulse records.
  • EIS evidence: frequency plus real and imaginary impedance, or a documented complex-impedance representation.
  • Join evidence: a reusable key that can link the time-series state to the EIS state.
  • DRT readiness: raw or reconstructable impedance, units, and a recorded quality-control step.

The compact queue is available as CSV for review and later ingestion.

Separate August source screen

Samsung broadband EIS source added to the candidate list

A separate source screen records a four-cell Samsung ICR18650-26J dataset with raw voltage/current acquisition and EIS measured across fixed SOC points. It is useful for a controlled TS/EIS alignment test, but it is not being claimed as a long-term ageing benchmark.

Priority queue

What to test first

Priority 1

Dynamic pulse to EIS benchmark

Published structure: 16 batteries, 14 tests per battery, one EIS trajectory and five dynamic-pulse profiles per test.

Why it fits: the source explicitly describes pulse profiles paired with the same EIS trajectory. This is the best immediate benchmark for the TS-to-EIS mapping component.

August file check: inspect the MATLAB structure, units, cell metadata, and licence before building a small adapter.

Open source record
Priority 2

LiPo EIS, capacity, and current-voltage data

Published structure: charge/discharge CSV files contain time, current, and voltage; EIS files are organised by cycle and SOC with frequency, Re(Z), and Im(Z).

Why it fits: its naming scheme appears to expose cycle and SOC keys on both sides of the data.

August file check: download a small sample, build a cycle-SOC manifest, and confirm that the EIS spectra can be reconstructed without guessing units.

Open source record
Priority 3

RWTH ageing and characterisation data

Published structure: high-resolution cycling data together with pulse tests, qOCV, capacity tests, and EIS at different temperatures; processed results describe 48 aged cells.

Why it fits: it has broad modality coverage and can support a more realistic ageing-oriented study.

August file check: inspect the raw directory structure and determine whether test-level identifiers link cycling and EIS records.

Open source record
EIS / DRT reference

Samsung INR18650-30Q ageing spectra

Published structure: eight cells aged under four profiles with capacity-fade and EIS measurements at fixed 25-cycle check-ups.

Why it matters: it is a useful EIS and DRT validation reference with SOC, SOH, OCV, and complex-impedance columns.

Limit: it should not be treated as a direct TS-to-EIS mapping source until a compatible pulse or high-resolution time-series side is confirmed.
