Samsung ICR18650-26J broadband EIS record

A controlled public record for checking whether raw voltage and current acquisition can be joined to EIS spectra at known state-of-charge points.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

What the source states

Published dataset facts

  • Cells: four Samsung ICR18650-26J cylindrical lithium-ion cells.
  • SOC coverage: EIS measurements at 100%, 90%, 80%, 70%, 60%, 50%, 40%, 30%, 20%, and 10% SOC.
  • Repeats: six repeated measurements per cell and SOC point are described on the landing page.
  • Files: raw voltage and current acquisition files are supplied in MATLAB format; the landing page points to a related dataset for computed EIS.
  • Licence: CC BY 4.0 is listed by the publisher.

Primary record: Buchicchio et al., Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/zdsgxwksn5.1.

Pipeline fit

Why this is useful, and where it stops

  • Useful for: checking a small adapter from raw voltage/current acquisition to a known SOC and EIS state.
  • Useful for: testing repeatability across cells and repeated measurements before a DRT workflow is applied.
  • Not yet suitable for: proving a long-term ageing, capacity-fade, or general cycling time-series to EIS mapping.
  • Reason: the published description centres on EIS acquisition at fixed SOC points, not on a full run-to-failure cycling experiment.
  • Decision: retain as a controlled TS/EIS alignment candidate and EIS/DRT quality-control reference.
Acceptance gate

Checks required before importing data

1. Verify the join

Confirm that the raw acquisition and computed EIS files share a cell identifier, SOC value, repeat number, and measurement timestamp or unambiguous filename key.

2. Verify impedance fields

Confirm frequency, real impedance, imaginary impedance, sign convention, and units. Do not infer missing columns from a plot.

3. Keep a raw manifest

Record source URL, file name, checksum if available, cell, SOC, repeat, protocol notes, and licence before transformation.

4. Run quality controls

Check duplicate spectra, frequency ordering, plausible impedance signs, and reconstruction residuals before any DRT comparison.

Next small experiment

One cell, two SOC points, one transparent result

Download the smallest usable subset after reviewing the publisher's files. Create a read-only manifest, reconstruct two spectra from their stored values, and report whether the file-level join works. A failed join is a useful research result and should be recorded rather than repaired by assumption.
