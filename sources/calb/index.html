CALB L148N58A comprehensive characterization study sources

Papers, dataset records, public files, and evidence limits behind the internal CALB pulse-to-EIS analysis.

CALB L148N58A comprehensive characterization studyEverythingExternal validation sources
Do not overread this: these are internal CALB experiment files. They support the internal pulse-bridge story, not external validation.

Paper title

A dataset for large prismatic lithium-ion battery cells (CALB L148N58A): Comprehensive characterization and real-world driving cycles

Open CALB paper

Dataset title

CALB L148N58A testing campaign. DOI: 10.17632/ycx459r5c3.2.

Open CALB source dataset

What this project did

Built an internal pulse-to-EIS analysis from selected HPPC and EIS outputs.

What this does not prove

It does not prove results on other datasets. Panasonic, RADAR4KIT, and Stanford do not support the fixed CALB rule.
