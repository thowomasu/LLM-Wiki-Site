Start here: how to read six versions

This was not one model improved six times on one fixed test. Each version changed the question after the previous evidence exposed a weakness. Scores are therefore not directly interchangeable.

Versions 1 to 3Established the task, added same-cell calibration, and tested portability. None earned external support.
Version 4Added impedance-scale normalization and achieved one narrow external pass.
Version 5Completed the missing-EIS-to-health chain, then failed to operate on SiCWell.
Version 6Tested rescue options across spent datasets and stopped the universal path.

The standard used throughout

A complicated model must beat a simple baseline under a split that keeps independent cells or datasets apart. Internal success supports development. Only a frozen result on untouched outside data supports an external claim.

Detailed comparison of Versions 4 to 6

The last three versions contain the most complete frozen evidence. They are still not three scores from one stable experiment. Each answers a harder question and changes the allowed claim.

Version 4

Can scale-normalized EIS and measured-EIS-to-SOH heads transfer to one tightly matched external dataset? Yes, narrowly.

Version 5

Can the complete predicted-EIS-to-SOH chain pass a new external dataset with a frozen guard? No.

Version 6

Can broader representations or tiny target-cell calibration rescue universal transfer? No candidate passed development gates.

Combined conclusion

The best next move is controlled exact-cell data, not another universal-model rescue attempt.

How the math evolved

Version 4: remove cell impedance scale and reconstruct change
\[s=|B_{1\,\mathrm{Hz}}|,\qquad \widehat{Z}_k=B_k+s\,f_k\left(\mathrm{SOC},\Delta R_{\mathrm{int}},B/s\right)\]
Version 5: feed predicted EIS into the health head
\[\widehat{h}_{V5}=g_c\left(\Delta R_{\mathrm{int}},\widehat{Z}/s,B/s\right),\qquad \widehat{Z}\text{ is cross-fitted by cell}\]
Version 6: compare representation and adaptation families by whole dataset
\[I_{d,h}=\frac{E_{d,h}^{\mathrm{baseline}}-E_{d,h}^{\mathrm{model}}}{E_{d,h}^{\mathrm{baseline}}},\qquad \widehat{y}_{\mathrm{adapt}}=a\widehat{y}_0+b\quad\text{or}\quad\widehat{y}_0+\overline{e}_C\]
Shared rule: improvement must survive the correct independent unit
\[\text{row metrics}\;\not\Rightarrow\;\text{cell validation}\;\not\Rightarrow\;\text{dataset validation}\]

Evidence hierarchy

RowA correlated measurement at one SOC or checkpoint. Never counted as an independent battery.
CellThe independent unit for same-dataset held-cell development and calibration budgets.
DatasetThe independent unit for protocol, chemistry, instrument, and lab transfer.
Untouched kept-back testA dataset that did not influence model, feature, threshold, or candidate selection.
Why V6 overrides attractive cell results:

A model may interpolate between cells inside one lab protocol and still fail when the entire dataset changes. Dataset-level LODO is the stronger test for the universal claim.

Why each version changed

V3 to V4: absolute scale was a transfer problem

V4 divided spectral shape and change by the fresh 1 Hz impedance magnitude.

V4 to V5: the health chain was incomplete

V5 trained SOH on EIS predicted without the held cell, removing measured current EIS from the required path.

V5 to V6: one guard failure was not enough diagnosis

V6 separated representations, heads, datasets, calibration budgets, abstention, and uncertainty to test whether anything was worth freezing.

After V6: the bottleneck is controlled evidence

More public-dataset searching cannot fix protocol and exact-cell mismatch. The next program needs planned measurements.

Current research decision

  • Keep V4 as narrow external support under its exact frozen Oxford contract.
  • Keep V5 as external_validation_failed with no external support.
  • Keep V6 as stop_universal_model_path with no model to validate.
  • Do not pool Leonardi and SiCWell as a generic NMC domain.
  • Continue only through the separate controlled exact-cell measurement gate.
